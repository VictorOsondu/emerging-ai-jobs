# Forward-Deployed AI Engineer

## Role Summary

A Forward-Deployed AI Engineer embeds with a customer's engineering team to design, build, and ship production AI systems on top of a vendor's models or platform. The role owns the path from discovery through deployment and iteration: finding the real workflow bottleneck, building an AI-powered solution against the customer's own systems and constraints, and staying accountable for its production quality.

## Why This Role Is Emerging Now

Enterprises are buying AI platforms faster than they can integrate them. Model capability is no longer the constraint; turning it into a reliable workflow inside a specific company's data, permissions, and processes is. AI vendors have adopted the forward-deployed pattern from data-platform consulting and made it a core delivery function, because agentic systems need engineering judgement applied inside the customer's context, not a handover document.

## Core Responsibilities

- Lead discovery with customer teams to identify high-value workflows and define success metrics.
- Design and build LLM- or agent-powered solutions integrated with customer systems.
- Ship fast first versions, then harden them with rollout plans, monitoring, and iteration.
- Own production quality: tracing, evaluations, model-behaviour debugging, latency and cost trade-offs, and failure modes.
- Feed reusable patterns and product gaps back to the vendor's core product and research teams.
- Act as the technical point of contact across the customer lifecycle, from pre-sales scoping to post-implementation support.

## Common Tools

- foundation-model APIs and agent frameworks
- evaluation and tracing tools
- cloud and customer-side infrastructure
- data integration and retrieval systems
- observability and monitoring stacks
- prototyping and deployment tooling

## Required Skills

- production software engineering
- applied LLM and agent-system design
- evaluation and reliability practices
- customer-facing discovery and communication
- working inside unfamiliar codebases and constraints
- scoping, prioritisation, and expectation management

## Adjacent Existing Roles

- solutions engineer or solutions architect
- professional-services or consulting engineer
- applied AI engineer or machine learning engineer
- customer engineer, sales engineer
- technical account manager

## What This Role Is Not

- Not a demo or pre-sales-only role; it owns systems running in production.
- Not a pure consulting engagement that ends at a recommendation document.
- Not a research role; it applies existing models rather than training new ones.
- Not the customer's internal platform team, although it works alongside one.

## Example Job Titles

- Forward Deployed Engineer
- Forward Deployed AI Engineer
- Forward Deployed Engineer, Agentic Platform
- Applied AI Engineer (forward-deployed)
- Forward Deployed Machine Learning Engineer

## Example Work Outputs

- a production agentic workflow integrated with a customer's systems
- evaluation suites and monitoring for deployed AI behaviour
- rollout, escalation, and failure-handling plans
- reusable solution patterns contributed back to the core product
- technical roadmaps agreed with customer engineering leadership

## Risks and Failure Modes

- Demos that never survive contact with production data, permissions, or scale.
- Success measured by launches rather than sustained workflow outcomes.
- Bespoke builds that cannot be maintained after the engineer rotates off.
- Vendor incentives overriding honest advice about where AI fits.
- Burnout from combining delivery pressure, travel, and on-call ownership.
