# AI Transformation Manager

## Role Summary

An AI Transformation Manager turns AI ambition into a managed portfolio of use cases, pilots, controls, adoption support, and measurable business change.

## Why This Role Is Emerging Now

Many organisations have executive AI interest but fragmented delivery. They need someone who can connect strategy, operations, technology, governance, and behaviour change without reducing adoption to tool rollout.

## Maturity

- Status: Formalising
- Confidence: Medium
- Source signal: Consulting and enterprise postings show recurring responsibilities around AI portfolio shaping, stakeholder orchestration, delivery oversight, and value realisation.

## Core Responsibilities

- Identify and prioritise AI use cases.
- Shape pilots into a coherent adoption portfolio.
- Coordinate business, data, technology, security, legal, and change teams.
- Maintain an adoption roadmap and decision rhythm across workstreams.
- Track benefits, risks, blockers, and adoption health.
- Prepare executive updates and decisions.

## Common Tools

- portfolio trackers
- business-case templates
- adoption dashboards
- workflow mapping tools
- AI readiness assessments
- collaboration and documentation tools

## Required Skills

- transformation delivery
- stakeholder management

- AI literacy
- governance awareness

## Adjacent Existing Roles

## What This Role Is Not

- Not just a project manager.
- Not usually the model builder.
- Not a one-person substitute for governance, data, security, or training.

## Example Job Titles

- AI Transformation Manager
- GenAI Transformation Lead
- AI Programme Manager
- AI Portfolio Lead
- AI Adoption Manager

## Example Work Outputs

- use-case portfolio
- pilot selection criteria
- AI adoption roadmap
- programme-owner charter
- value realisation dashboard
- stakeholder decision log
- rollout risk register

## Risks and Failure Modes

- Too many pilots and no operating change.
- Benefits tracked as activity, not outcomes.
- Governance bolted on late.
- Executive ambition outpaces user support.
