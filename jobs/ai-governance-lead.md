# AI Governance Lead

## Role Summary

The AI Governance Lead owns the operating system for responsible AI use: policy, risk tiering, approvals, inventories, controls, and reporting, plus coordination across legal, compliance, privacy, security, and the business.

## Why This Role Is Emerging Now

AI use has moved from isolated experimentation into enterprise workflows. Organisations now need someone to translate AI regulation, risk frameworks, procurement concerns, and responsible-use principles into practical controls that teams can follow.

## Classification

- Role origin: Hybrid
- Operating model: AI-augmented
- Maturity: Formalising
- Confidence: Medium
- Source signal: Multiple recent exact-title postings and governance frameworks point to this role shape, although no exact-title posting is currently verifiable as open.

## Core Responsibilities

- Maintain AI governance policies, standards, and approval routes.
- Own the AI system inventory and risk-tiering process.
- Coordinate AI governance forums or review boards.
- Map controls to privacy, security, legal, procurement, and audit expectations.
- Report AI risk, adoption, exceptions, and incidents to leadership.

## Common Tools

- AI inventory or model registry tools
- GRC platforms
- vendor-risk systems
- policy and documentation tools
- DPIA, FRIA, and risk-assessment templates
- evaluation and monitoring dashboards

## Required Skills

- governance, risk, compliance, privacy, or legal operations
- AI lifecycle literacy
- stakeholder management
- policy design
- risk assessment
- executive reporting

## Adjacent Existing Roles

- Data Governance Lead
- Privacy Lead
- Technology Risk Manager
- Responsible AI Lead
- Model Risk Manager
- Compliance Programme Manager

## What This Role Is Not

- Not usually the person building models.
- Not the sole owner of all AI risk.
- Not a blocker function if designed well.

## Example Job Titles

- AI Governance Lead
- Responsible AI Lead
- Head of AI Governance
- AI Governance and Compliance Manager
- AI Risk Governance Lead

## Example Work Outputs

- AI use policy
- AI system inventory
- risk-tiering model
- approval workflow
- vendor AI due-diligence checklist
- board AI governance dashboard

## Risks and Failure Modes

- Governance becomes paperwork without adoption.
- Low-risk use cases are slowed down unnecessarily.
- High-risk use cases avoid review.
- Ownership is unclear between legal, security, data, and product.
