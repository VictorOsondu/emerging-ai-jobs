# AI Enablement Lead

## Role Summary

An AI Enablement Lead helps people adopt AI safely and usefully through training, workflow examples, support channels, communities of practice, and reusable guidance.

## Why This Role Is Emerging Now

Tool access alone does not create adoption. Organisations need role-relevant examples, safe-use habits, peer support, and a way to convert early wins into repeatable practice.

## Maturity

- Status: Formalising
- Confidence: High
- Source signal: Multiple current exact-title postings plus a first-party organisational programme description.

## Core Responsibilities

- Design AI literacy and role-based learning.
- Build approved workflow examples.
- Run office hours, clinics, or champion networks.
- Coordinate communities of practice and turn repeated questions into reusable guidance.
- Translate policy into practical guidance.
- Track adoption blockers and support needs.

## What This Role Is Not

- Not just training delivery.
- Not a helpdesk for every AI issue.
- Not a substitute for governance or policy ownership.

## Risks and Failure Modes

- Training is too generic to change work.
- Enthusiasts race ahead without guardrails.
- Staff feel pressured rather than supported.
- Enablement is not connected to policy or approved tools.
