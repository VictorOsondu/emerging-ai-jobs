# AI Product Operations Manager

## Role Summary

An AI Product Operations Manager builds the operating layer around AI products: release readiness, eval coordination, feedback loops, incident intake, and documentation. Much of the job is keeping product, engineering, support, and legal in sync.

## Why This Role Is Emerging Now

AI products behave less predictably than traditional software features. Product teams need operational discipline around evaluation, monitoring, human review, customer feedback, model changes, and quality regressions.

## Maturity

- Status: Formalising
- Confidence: Medium
- Source signal: Product-operations roles now recur at AI companies, although the `AI Product Operations Manager` title is not yet consistent.

## Core Responsibilities

- Coordinate AI feature release readiness.
- Maintain feedback and incident loops for AI behaviours.
- Track eval, quality, and support signals.
- Document model, prompt, retrieval, and policy changes.
- Help product teams convert user feedback into model or workflow improvements.

## Common Tools

- product analytics
- eval dashboards
- issue trackers
- customer-support tools
- release-management systems
- model/prompt documentation tools

## Required Skills

- product operations
- AI literacy
- cross-functional coordination
- quality metrics
- incident handling

## What This Role Is Not

- Not the sole product decision-maker.
- Not a replacement for engineering ownership.
- Not just support escalation.

## Example Job Titles

- AI Product Operations Manager
- AI Product Ops Lead
- GenAI Product Operations Manager
- AI Quality Operations Manager
- AI Release Operations Lead

## Example Work Outputs

- AI release checklist
- eval readiness tracker
- model-change communication plan
- AI incident intake process
- customer-feedback synthesis
- quality regression report

## Risks and Failure Modes

- Product ships without evaluation ownership.
- Customer reports are treated as isolated tickets.
- Model changes break existing workflows.
- No one owns the operating cadence after launch.

## Public Signals

- OpenAI and Mistral currently recruit Product Operations Managers for launch readiness, release cadence, feedback synthesis, cross-functional coordination, and AI-assisted internal systems ([PRODOPS-01 and PRODOPS-02](../sources.md#evidence-register)).
- The work is recurrent enough to call the role family formalising, but the `AI Product Operations Manager` label remains inconsistent.
