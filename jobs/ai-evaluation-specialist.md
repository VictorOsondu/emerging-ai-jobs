# AI Evaluation Specialist

## Role Summary

An AI Evaluation Specialist builds the quality and safety layer for AI systems: eval datasets, rubrics, automated checks, red-team tests, monitoring, and improvement loops.

## Why This Role Is Emerging Now

LLM and agent systems can fail subtly. Teams need repeatable ways to tell whether a change improved or degraded accuracy, safety, grounding, and usefulness.

## Maturity

- Status: Formalising
- Confidence: High
- Source signal: Public job postings and specialist hiring pages increasingly describe evaluation, rubrics, model-response review, red-teaming, monitoring, and quality systems.

## Core Responsibilities

- Design evaluation datasets and rubrics.
- Build automated and human review workflows.
- Test prompts, retrieval, models, and agents against expected behaviour.
- Run red-team and edge-case testing.
- Track regressions, drift, and quality trends.
- Turn findings into product, prompt, data, or model improvements.

## What This Role Is Not

- Not only rating model answers for training data.
- Not just prompt engineering.
- Not a one-off pre-launch check.

## Risks and Failure Modes

- Measuring what is easy instead of what matters.
- Treating model-response contractor work as the whole discipline.
- Letting LLM-as-judge scores replace human judgement.
- Eval data grows stale as product use changes.
