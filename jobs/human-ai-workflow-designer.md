# Human-AI Workflow Designer

## Role Summary

A Human-AI Workflow Designer redesigns tasks so humans and AI systems each do the parts they are suited for, with clear handoffs, review points, escalation paths, and accountability.

## Why This Role Is Emerging Now

AI is changing workflows, not just individual tasks. Organisations need people who can decide where AI should assist, where humans must review, and how work should move between tools, people, and automated steps.

## Maturity

- Status: Emerging
- Confidence: Early signal
- Source signal: The title is not yet common, but the work appears across service design, operations, automation, AI product, and transformation roles.

## Core Responsibilities

- Map current workflows and pain points.
- Identify where AI should assist, automate, or stay out.
- Design human review and exception handling.
- Prototype AI-assisted workflows.
- Document roles, data boundaries, and escalation paths.

## What This Role Is Not

- Not a generic prompt role.
- Not pure automation.
- Not a way to remove human accountability from high-stakes work.

## Example Work Outputs

- current and future workflow maps

- AI suitability matrix
- escalation map
- workflow pilot brief
- review checklist

## Risks and Failure Modes

- Automating a broken process.
- Removing human review where judgement is needed.
- Ignoring data sensitivity.
- Designing workflows that staff cannot realistically follow.
