# AI Risk and Assurance Analyst

## Role Summary

An AI Risk and Assurance Analyst reviews AI systems, controls, documentation, testing, and evidence to help an organisation understand whether AI use is operating within policy, risk appetite, and external obligations.

## Why This Role Is Emerging Now

AI governance creates evidence requirements: inventories, model cards, risk assessments, evaluation records, human-review controls, vendor documentation, and incident histories. Assurance work turns those artefacts into confidence or findings.

## Maturity

- Status: Formalising
- Confidence: Medium
- Source signal: Responsibilities are visible across AI governance, model risk, audit, compliance, and responsible AI roles.

## Core Responsibilities

- Review AI risk assessments and control evidence.
- Test whether governance processes are followed.
- Check documentation completeness.
- Support internal audit or second-line assurance.
- Track exceptions, remediation, and recurring control gaps.

## Common Tools

- GRC systems
- AI inventory tools
- risk registers
- audit workpaper tools
- eval reports
- vendor-risk platforms

## Required Skills

- risk and controls
- audit evidence
- AI lifecycle literacy
- regulatory awareness
- documentation review
- analytical writing

## Adjacent Existing Roles

- IT Risk Analyst
- Internal Auditor
- Model Risk Analyst
- Compliance Analyst
- Data Protection Analyst
- AI Governance Analyst

## What This Role Is Not

- Not the owner of every AI decision.
- Not a model developer.
- Not a guarantee that AI is risk-free.

## Example Job Titles

- AI Risk Analyst
- AI Assurance Analyst
- Responsible AI Assurance Analyst
- AI Controls Analyst
- AI Governance Analyst

## Example Work Outputs

- assurance review notes
- control testing evidence
- AI documentation gap report
- remediation tracker
- exception summary
- audit-ready evidence pack

## Risks and Failure Modes

- Assurance becomes box-ticking.
- Technical claims are accepted without testing evidence.
- Controls exist on paper but not in workflows.
- Findings do not reach accountable owners.
