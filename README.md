## Who this is for

- **Professionals** trying to understand which AI-adjacent roles are real enough to prepare for.
- **Leaders and hiring managers** trying to write clearer job descriptions.
- **L&D and career teams** trying to map skills, training, and role transitions.
- **Researchers and operators** tracking how AI changes work.

## How to read the catalogue

Every role has:

- responsibilities
- common tools
- skills
- adjacent roles
- example outputs
- what the role is not
- source signals
- maturity status
- confidence level

### Maturity status

- **Emerging** — early public signals, inconsistent title, often bundled into another role.
- **Formalising** — repeated public job postings or org patterns, but scope still varies.
- **Established** — common enough that responsibilities and seniority bands are becoming recognisable.

### Confidence level

- **High** — multiple public job postings or standards support the role shape.
- **Medium** — clear signals exist, but title and scope are still inconsistent.
- **Early signal** — plausible role, but mostly visible in founder/operator writing or niche teams.

## Emerging job playbooks

Playbooks come after the role catalogue. A profile answers "what is this role?" A playbook goes further: how do you become useful in it, hire for it, or stand up the function?

The first playbooks should cover the roles with the strongest public signal:

- AI Governance Lead
- AI Evaluation Specialist
- AI Transformation Manager

See [playbooks/README.md](playbooks/README.md) for the playbook standard.

## Contribution standard

Use [templates/job-profile-template.md](templates/job-profile-template.md) for new roles and [templates/role-comparison-template.md](templates/role-comparison-template.md) when two titles are being confused.

A role should not be added because it sounds plausible. It needs at least one public signal:

- job posting
- company career page
- public org chart or team description
- analyst or standards reference
- credible practitioner write-up
- repeated evidence across adjacent job descriptions

## Scope

This repository is public career and workforce mapping. It is not career advice, hiring advice, immigration advice, compensation advice, legal advice, or a guarantee that a role title will persist.

Role boundaries will change quickly. Treat each profile as a dated market snapshot, not a permanent definition.
