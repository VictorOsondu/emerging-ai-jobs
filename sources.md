# Sources and Verification Notes

Last reviewed: 2026-07-25.

This repository is practitioner-built and source-informed. It tracks emerging AI roles from public job postings, standards, guidance, and practitioner writing. It is not a labour-market forecast, salary benchmark, or exhaustive employment dataset.

The GitHub AI Adoption Playbook by GitHub is used and adapted with changes under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Attribution does not imply GitHub endorsement of this catalogue.

## Source Quality

- **Primary signal** — current job posting, company career page, official standard, regulator guidance, or company role description.
- **Secondary signal** — analyst report, professional body material, credible market guide, or repeated recruiter descriptions.
- **Early signal** — practitioner essays, founder/operator posts, or niche role descriptions that have not yet become common job postings.

## Interpretation

- Multiple current postings support `High` confidence for AI governance, AI evaluation, AI enablement, and prompt engineering.
- A single exact-title posting plus recurring adjacent responsibilities supports `Medium`, not `High`, confidence for AI transformation and agent operations.
- AI product operations and synthetic-data work recur, but titles vary enough to remain below `High` confidence.
- Human-AI workflow design remains an `Early signal`: the responsibility bundle is credible, but exact-title hiring evidence is not yet recurrent.

## Verification Boundaries

- Job titles change faster than responsibilities. Profiles group similar work, not exact titles.
- A single job posting is not enough to mark a role established.
- Live postings can close or move. Evidence should be rechecked quarterly and replaced with an archived or current first-party source when a link expires.
- A first-party programme description supports the shape of the work but does not prove that its title or organisational design is standard across employers.
- Contractor model-rating jobs and production AI evaluation jobs should not be treated as the same career path.
- Salary claims are intentionally excluded from the first version.
- This is not an endorsement of any employer, vendor, certification, or tool.
