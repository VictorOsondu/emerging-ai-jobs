# Research Analyst: AI-Augmented Evolution

## Evolution Summary

An AI-augmented Research Analyst uses AI to accelerate data exploration, code generation, source discovery, comparison, and early synthesis. Human effort moves towards framing questions, testing provenance, choosing methods, resolving contradictions, and turning evidence into defensible conclusions.

## Why the Work Is Changing Now

Language models can work across text, code, tables, and large document collections in one workflow. Current evidence shows these tools becoming a normal analytical expectation, but not yet a reliable substitute for source verification, causal reasoning, or accountable recommendations.

## Classification

- Role origin: Existing
- Operating model: AI-augmented
- Maturity: Formalising
- Confidence: Medium

## Evolution of the Workflow

| Work component | Traditional model | AI-augmented model | Human responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Question framing | Analyst converts a brief into research questions | AI proposes decompositions, assumptions, and missing questions | Select the framing and expose value judgements |
| Discovery | Analyst searches sources and datasets sequentially | AI broadens queries, clusters material, and identifies candidate evidence | Verify provenance and decide what is admissible |
| Analysis | Analyst writes most queries and exploratory code | AI generates candidate code, transformations, and hypotheses | Validate methods, data quality, and causal claims |
| Synthesis | Analyst drafts from notes and outputs | AI produces comparisons and provisional structures | Resolve contradictions and build the argument |
| Review | Peer review focuses on the final output | Review also inspects citations, transformations, prompts, and unsupported inference | Own traceability and the published conclusion |

## Tasks Increasing in Importance

- research design and question quality
- source provenance and citation verification
- checking generated code and transformations
- distinguishing correlation, causation, and speculation
- documenting how AI contributed to an output
- communicating uncertainty and limitations

## Tasks Decreasing or Being Delegated

- initial query expansion
- repetitive cleaning and formatting
- first-pass code and chart generation
- document clustering and summarisation
- mechanical comparison across many sources

## New Skills and Judgement

- AI-assisted research design
- data and source provenance
- generated-code review
- adversarial fact checking
- privacy and confidential-data handling
- uncertainty communication

## Tools and Systems

- language-model research assistants
- notebooks and analytical databases
- retrieval and document-analysis tools
- citation and reference managers
- reproducible data pipelines
- versioned research logs

## Measures of Good Work

- traceable claims with admissible sources
- reproducibility of analysis and transformations
- correction and unsupported-claim rate
- time from question to reviewed insight
- quality of uncertainty and limitation reporting
- decision usefulness rather than output volume

## Human Accountability

The analyst remains accountable for source selection, methodological choices, confidential-data handling, factual accuracy, causal interpretation, and recommendations. AI-generated citations, code, or findings require verification against the underlying material.

## Transition Path

Start with query expansion, code assistance, and bounded document comparison. Preserve a research log linking claims to sources and transformations. Expand delegation only when reviewers can reproduce the work without relying on an opaque conversation history.

## Risks and Failure Modes

- Fabricated or misattributed sources enter the evidence base.
- Generated analysis contains silent methodological errors.
- Fast synthesis creates confidence before understanding.
- Sensitive material is sent to an unsuitable system.
- Reviewers see polished prose without a reproducible trail.
